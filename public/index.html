What this scenario does—and does not—cover

It creates a transparent starting point for a scoping conversation. A complete project estimate may also require:

  • project management, stakeholder time, procurement, change orders, and contingency;
  • model and API usage, hosting, storage, security, privacy, accessibility, evaluation, monitoring, and incident response;
  • data acquisition or licensing, subcontractors, travel, ongoing support, maintenance, taxes, and professional review.

No preset demonstrates compliance or substitutes for discovery. Your entries stay in this browser; exporting creates a local CSV. This page does not collect or send scenario data.
